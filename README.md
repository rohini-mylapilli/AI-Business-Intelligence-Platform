\# AI Business Intelligence Platform



\## Project Overview



AI Business Intelligence Platform is a full-stack business analytics application that helps businesses manage and analyze their sales, inventory, customers, employees, expenses, and overall business performance from a centralized platform.



The platform will provide data analytics, machine learning predictions, and AI-powered business recommendations.
